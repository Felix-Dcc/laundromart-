Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LaundroMart ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the LaundroMart mobile application and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and phone number, provided when you register.
  • Addresses — the pickup and delivery addresses you enter for your orders.
  • Location data — with your permission, your device's precise (GPS) location, used to show nearby laundromats and to display your delivery rider's live position on a map. Riders share their location only while an order is active.
  • Order information — your service requests, laundry details, order history, and ratings you submit.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by our payment provider, Paystack. Card details are entered directly into Paystack's secure flow; we do not store your card numbers. We retain a record of transactions (amount, status, reference) for your order history.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To connect you with laundry providers and delivery riders, and to fulfil, price, and deliver your orders.
  • To show nearby providers and enable live rider tracking.
  • To process payments and maintain your order and payment history.
  • To send you notifications about your order status.
  • To provide customer support and improve the Service.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as needed to operate the Service:

  • Laundry providers and riders — receive the order details necessary to collect, process, and deliver your laundry (e.g. name, addresses, and, during an active order, your location).
  • Service providers acting on our behalf — Paystack (payment processing), Google Maps Platform (maps, geocoding, and directions), and push-notification services (Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service).
  • Legal reasons — where required by law or to protect our rights and users.

4. Data Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted, hashed values. We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time:

  • In the app — go to Profile → Delete Account.
  • By email — contact us at the address below and we will delete your account and personal data, subject to any records we are legally required to keep.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
