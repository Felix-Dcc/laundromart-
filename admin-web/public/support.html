Support

LaundroMart connects you with nearby laundromats for on-demand laundry pickup and delivery, with live rider tracking and payment by mobile money or card. This page explains how to get help.

Help with your order

How do I place an order?

Open the app, allow location access, and choose a laundromat from the map or list. Select a service, enter an estimated weight and your pickup address, choose a pickup time, and confirm. A nearby rider is then dispatched to collect your laundry.

Why is my final price different from the estimate?

The price you see when ordering is an estimate based on the weight you entered. When your laundry reaches the laundromat it is weighed, and the final price is calculated from the actual weight at the laundromat's published rate. You are shown the verified weight and the final amount before you are asked to pay, and no processing begins until you have approved and paid.

My order has not been picked up

Orders are published to nearby riders and are accepted on a first-come basis. At busy times this can take longer than usual. If your order has been waiting an unusually long time, email us with your order number and we will follow it up with the laundromat and riders.

How do I track my order?

Open Orders and select the order. You will see a status timeline covering every stage from pickup to delivery, and your rider's live position on a map while they are on the way.

Payments and refunds

Payments are processed securely by Paystack, supporting mobile money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo) and debit or credit cards. LaundroMart does not store your card number.

If a payment was deducted but your order does not show as paid, wait a few minutes for confirmation to arrive, then email us with your order number and the payment reference from your receipt. If an order is cancelled after payment, or laundry is lost or damaged, contact us and we will investigate and arrange a refund where appropriate.

Account and data

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app: go to Profile → Delete my account and confirm with your password. This permanently removes your account and personal data. You may also request deletion by emailing oseipokufelix0@gmail.com from your registered address.

Reporting a problem with the app

If something is not working, please tell us:

  • what you were trying to do,

  • the order number, if the problem relates to a specific order.
